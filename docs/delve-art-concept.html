DELVE THE BLACK LABYRINTH

Art concept v4, not the game. A labyrinth of rooms where each room you enter is a stage and something happens to you.

TV VIEW

Left: the room you're in, shown from the side. Your hero is on the left and whatever lives in the room is on the right. The three archways are this room's doors (west, north, east). Right: the room map. Icons mark visited rooms, ? marks a door you've seen but not gone through, and your current room glows.
